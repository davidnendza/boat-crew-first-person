Role levels

Each crewmate also earns experience in four roles. A role level is separate from the six attributes and the two are combined in the speed and rate calculations.

Captain

  • Level feeds the craft handling offset, together with hand-eye coordination.

Medic

  • Level feeds heal speed, together with hand-eye coordination.

Repairman

  • Level feeds repair speed, together with hand-eye coordination.

Gunner

  • Level feeds the gun's turn rate and its aim code, and the game defines a shot-cooldown bonus per gunner level.
  • Reload speed also reads a role level, probably this one.

"Gunnery" means two different things

GUNNER ROLE LEVEL

How well this crewmate shoots

Comes from the crewmate's experience as a gunner. It speeds up how fast they swing a gun, shortens the wait between shots, and goes into the gun's aim calculation next to hand-eye coordination. Each weapon also carries its own sway multiplier, so how steady a shot is depends on the crewmate and on the gun.

GUNNERY SCORE

A campaign damage bonus

A progression value for your whole fleet, not a crewmate. It is converted into extra damage when a gun fires, and two campaign unlocks add damage effects on top. It makes hits stronger and has nothing to do with aim.

What this page cannot tell you

  • How much. One point of any stat has a real effect size, and I have not read the formulas or the numbers.
  • Which way each effect runs. Faster reloading, steadier aim and a longer survival time are clearly good. For run cooldown I assume a higher stat helps, from the name alone.
  • What "live" means. Each stat has a raw value, a trait adjustment, an effective value and a live value. The live one is what most calculations use. It is probably affected by current condition, which I have not confirmed.
  • How big the adaptability effect is. It clearly feeds the XP gain rate, but I have not read how many points of it change the rate, or how much XP each level costs.
  • Cooperativeness. It feeds a role-contribution score and an ability weight. That points at how crew are ranked or assigned, and I cannot say more.
