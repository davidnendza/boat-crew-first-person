Role levels

CaptainLevel feeds the craft handling offset, together with hand-eye coordination.
MedicLevel feeds heal speed, together with hand-eye coordination.
RepairmanLevel feeds repair speed, together with hand-eye coordination.
GunnerLevel feeds gun turn rate and the aim code, and there is a shot-cooldown bonus per level. Reload speed also reads a role level, probably this one.

"Gunnery" is two things

GUNNER ROLE LEVEL

How well this crewmate shoots

From the crewmate's gunner experience. Faster gun swing, shorter wait between shots, and part of the aim calculation next to hand-eye coordination. Each weapon also has its own sway multiplier.

GUNNERY SCORE

A campaign damage bonus

A progression value for your whole fleet, converted into extra damage when a gun fires. Two campaign unlocks add damage effects on top. It strengthens hits and has nothing to do with aim.

Not known: the sizes of the effects (no formulas read), whether a higher stat always helps (run cooldown assumed), and what "live" adds to "effective". Adaptability feeding the XP gain rate is confirmed in the code. Cooperativeness only feeds a role-contribution score and an ability weight.
